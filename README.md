### Profile

My work brings together communication, information, technology and research, with a particular focus on security, responsible digital practices and information integrity. I approach these fields from both strategic and analytical perspectives, with particular attention to how information is created, communicated, governed and used.

I value precision, intellectual discipline and ethical responsibility. These principles guide the way I work, particularly where technology and information intersect with people, organisations and society.

### Areas of Work

**Strategic Communication**  
Communication strategy, institutional positioning and governance.

**Information Governance & Privacy**  
Information governance, privacy and responsible data practices.

**Cybersecurity & Digital Responsibility**  
Cybersecurity awareness, digital literacy, information integrity and responsible technology practices.

**Research & Analysis**  
Research, critical analysis, data assessment and knowledge development.

### Principles

I believe that technology and information should be approached with precision, responsibility and respect for their wider impact.

Integrity matters as much as capability. I value critical thinking, responsible communication, information integrity and the careful use of technology — particularly where digital systems affect people, organisations and society.

### Background

Portuguese, born in Mafra, Portugal, with an academic and professional background spanning communication, information, technology and research.

**Professional**  
Chief Communications Officer at TELENIMA, with a focus on institutional strategy and communication.

**Academic**  
Academic background in communication and related fields, with studies at the University of Lisbon and the University of Barcelona; currently pursuing doctoral research.

**Languages**  
Portuguese (native) · English · Spanish

Selected writing and research on communication, technology, cybersecurity, information and society.
